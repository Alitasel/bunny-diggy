# Privacy Policy for Bunny Diggy
**Last updated: August 13, 2026**
Bunny Diggy ("the App") is a casual puzzle game developed and published by the developer of Bunny Diggy ("we", "us", or "our").
This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Bunny Diggy.
## 1. Information We Collect
Bunny Diggy is designed primarily as a single-player game and does not require users to create an account with us.
We do not directly collect or maintain personal information such as your name, home address, phone number, or personal email address through Bunny Diggy.
However, certain third-party services integrated into the App may collect or process information as described below.
## 2. Advertising
Bunny Diggy uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements, including rewarded advertisements.
Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:
- Device identifiers
- Advertising identifiers, where permitted
- IP address
- Device and operating system information
- Advertising interaction information
- Diagnostic and performance information
- Approximate location derived from information such as an IP address, where applicable
The information processed by Google may be used to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising, subject to applicable law and the user's privacy choices.
Where required by applicable law, Bunny Diggy uses Google's consent management tools to request or manage user consent before requesting certain advertising services.
For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's privacy policies and information about how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
## 3. In-App Purchases
Bunny Diggy offers an optional in-app purchase that allows users to remove advertising.
In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.
We do not directly receive or store your payment card information or Apple Account payment credentials.
Apple may process transaction and account information in accordance with Apple's own privacy policies.
## 4. Game Center
Bunny Diggy may use Apple Game Center features, such as leaderboards.
When you use Game Center, information associated with your Game Center account and gameplay activity may be processed by Apple according to Apple's privacy policies.
We do not receive your Apple Account password or payment credentials.
## 5. Game Progress and Local Data
Game progress, settings, earned items, and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on your device.
Deleting the App may remove locally stored game data unless that information is separately maintained by an Apple service or another feature explicitly provided by the App.
## 6. Tracking and Advertising Choices
Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be presented with privacy or consent choices relating to advertising.
Where required, you may be able to consent to, reject, or manage certain advertising data processing through the privacy controls presented in the App.
You may also manage certain privacy and advertising permissions through your device settings.
## 7. Children's Privacy
Bunny Diggy is intended for a general audience.
We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
Third-party services used by the App may process information according to their own policies and applicable legal requirements.
If we become aware that personal information has been collected in a manner inconsistent with applicable children's privacy laws, we will take appropriate steps to address the issue.
## 8. Third-Party Services
Bunny Diggy may use services provided by third parties, including:
- Google AdMob
- Google User Messaging Platform
- Apple App Store and StoreKit
- Apple Game Center
These third-party services operate according to their own terms and privacy policies.
## 9. International Users
Bunny Diggy may be available in multiple countries and regions.
Where required by applicable privacy laws, users may be provided with additional privacy choices regarding advertising and data processing.
## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.
Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
