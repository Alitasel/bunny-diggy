# Bunny Diggy

## Dig. Calculate. Find the Carrot!

Bunny Diggy is a cheerful number puzzle adventure where every carrot is hidden behind a mathematical challenge.

Connect numbers and operators, calculate the correct result, and guide your path to the carrot.

## Features

### Hundreds of Puzzle Levels

Explore increasingly challenging number puzzles across the Carrot Fields.

### Simple Rules, Clever Challenges

Connect adjacent tiles to build mathematical expressions and reach the target value.

### Multi-Board Puzzles

Advanced levels introduce multiple puzzle boards whose results combine into one final target.

### Special Runes

Discover new mechanics and special tiles as you progress through the game.

### Daily Challenge

Race against the clock and complete as many puzzles as possible.

Compete for a place on the Daily Challenge leaderboard.

### Level Ratings

Earn S, A, B, or C ratings based on your route, hints, and cancels.

Replay levels to improve your result and collect more carrots.

### Collect Carrots

Build your carrot collection as you complete puzzles and earn leaderboard rewards.

### Game Center Leaderboards

Compare your progress with other players through:

- Highest Level
- Total Carrots
- Daily Challenge

## Play Your Way

Bunny Diggy combines casual puzzle gameplay with mental arithmetic and logic challenges in a bright and relaxing carrot-farm adventure.
